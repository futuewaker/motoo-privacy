感谢你使用 Motoo。Motoo 是一款「本地优先」的记忆类 App —— 你拍下的瞬间、抠出的贴纸、归集的藏馆，默认都保存在你的设备本地。 我们非常重视你的隐私，本政策说明 Motoo 如何处理你的信息。

一、我们如何处理你的信息

Motoo 的核心功能（拍摄、AI 抠图、玻璃球收藏、日历与藏馆回看） 默认在你的设备上本地完成。个人玻璃球与藏馆数据通过 iCloud（CloudKit） 在你的 Apple 账号设备间同步， 不会经过 Motoo 自有服务器。

例外：多人「共享藏馆」功能（设置 → 我们的球 / 珍藏页共享藏馆）。若你主动创建或加入共享藏馆：

  • 共享藏馆内的贴纸图片会上传至我们的云服务（Supabase Storage）以便与其他成员同步；
  • 你的 Motoo 账号标识（Sign in with Apple）、设备推送 token 会保存在 Supabase，用于向成员发送「TA 封存了贴纸」等通知；
  • 若你设置头像，头像图片可能上传至 Supabase Storage；
  • 该功能需登录；不登录时个人玻璃球仍可正常使用。

具体涉及：

  • 照片与图像：当你用相机拍摄或从相册导入时，图像与抠图结果保存在你设备的本地存储中。 抠图（主体识别）由系统的设备端 Vision 框架完成，不经过网络。
  • 位置信息（可选）：若你授权定位，Motoo 会在你拍摄时记录瞬间的坐标，用于「地图回看」。 我们也会读取你所选照片的 EXIF 中已包含的 GPS 信息用于同一目的。位置信息 仅保存在本地，不会上传。
  • 本地用户档案：昵称、头像、会员状态等保存在设备本地；共享功能相关的账号信息按上文说明同步至 Supabase。

二、设备权限说明

Motoo 会在需要时向你申请以下权限，你可随时在系统「设置」中关闭：

权限用途
相机拍摄要封存的瞬间
相册 / 照片导入已有照片、（可选）导出贴纸到相册
定位（可选）记录瞬间坐标用于地图回看
通知（可选）共享藏馆成员活动提醒

三、数据存储与同步

  • 本地存储：你的个人内容默认仅存于本设备。
  • iCloud 同步（如开启）：若你启用跨设备同步，数据会通过 你自己的 Apple iCloud 账户在你的设备间同步。该过程由 Apple iCloud 提供并受 Apple 隐私政策 约束，我们无法访问你 iCloud 中的内容。
  • 共享藏馆（可选）：仅当你创建或加入共享藏馆时，相关贴纸与账号信息才会上传至 Supabase（见上文）。

四、购买信息

会员订阅与购买通过 Apple App Store 处理。我们 不会收到或存储你的支付方式、银行卡号等支付信息；相关交易由 Apple 依其条款与隐私政策处理。

五、我们不做的事

  • 我们不收集用于追踪你的个人身份信息，也不要求注册账户即可使用核心功能。
  • 我们不接入第三方广告或分析 SDK 来收集你的使用数据。
  • 我们不出售、不出租你的个人信息给任何第三方。

六、儿童隐私

Motoo 不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

七、你的权利与选择

  • 删除数据：你可在 App 内删除单条瞬间或藏馆； 卸载 App 即会清除设备本地的全部 Motoo 数据。
  • 关闭权限：可随时在系统「设置」关闭相机 / 相册 / 定位 / 通知授权。
  • 管理 iCloud 数据：如启用同步，可在「设置 → Apple 账户 → iCloud」中管理。
  • 退出共享藏馆：可在 App 内退出或解散共享藏馆；退出后不再接收该藏馆的云端同步。

八、政策更新

我们可能不时更新本政策。重大变更会在本页更新「生效日期」，并在适当情况下于 App 内提示。

English

Thank you for using Motoo. Motoo is a local-first memory app — the moments you capture, stickers you create, and collections you organize are stored on your device by default. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how Motoo handles your information.

1. How We Handle Your Information

Core Motoo features (capture, on-device AI cutout, glass ball collections, calendar and gallery review) run locally on your device by default. Personal glass ball and collection data syncs across your Apple devices via iCloud (CloudKit) and does not pass through Motoo-owned servers.

Exception: Shared Collections (Settings → Our Ball / shared collections in Gallery). If you create or join a shared collection:

  • Sticker images in the shared collection are uploaded to our cloud service (Supabase Storage) so members can view and sync them;
  • Your Motoo account identifier (Sign in with Apple) and device push token are stored in Supabase to send activity notifications (e.g., when someone adds a sticker);
  • If you set a profile avatar, the avatar image may be uploaded to Supabase Storage;
  • This feature requires sign-in. Your personal glass ball works without an account.

Details:

  • Photos & images: When you capture or import photos, images and cutout results are stored locally. Cutout (subject segmentation) uses the on-device Vision framework and does not use the network.
  • Location (optional): If you grant location access, Motoo records coordinates when you capture moments for map review. We may also read GPS from photo EXIF for the same purpose. Location data stays on device and is not uploaded.
  • Local profile: Display name, avatar, and membership status are stored locally; account data for shared features is synced to Supabase as described above.

2. Device Permissions

Motoo requests the following permissions when needed. You can revoke them anytime in iOS Settings:

PermissionPurpose
CameraCapture moments to seal
PhotosImport photos; optionally export stickers to your library
Location(Optional) Record coordinates for map review
Notifications(Optional) Alerts for shared collection activity

3. Storage & Sync

  • Local storage: Your personal content stays on your device by default.
  • iCloud sync (optional): If enabled, data syncs across your devices via your Apple iCloud account, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access your iCloud data.
  • Shared collections (optional): Relevant stickers and account data are uploaded to Supabase only when you create or join a shared collection (see above).

4. Purchases

Membership subscriptions and purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store payment card details; Apple handles transactions under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. What We Don't Do

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information for tracking, and you can use core features without creating an account.
  • We do not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs to collect usage data.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Motoo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

7. Your Choices

  • Delete data: Delete individual moments or collections in the app; uninstalling removes all local Motoo data.
  • Revoke permissions: Turn off camera, photos, location, or notifications in iOS Settings anytime.
  • Manage iCloud: Control synced data in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud.
  • Leave shared collections: Leave or dissolve a shared collection in the app to stop cloud sync for that collection.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will update the effective date on this page and may be noted in the app when appropriate.
